# 💰 Business Model & Unit Economics: KROH (ក្រោះ)
> **The Dual B2C Civic Good & B2B Threat Intelligence API Engine**  
> **Team:** Team CHBAS (ច្បាស់ ច្បាស់) — AUPP - ITM Innovation Hackathon 2026

---

## 1. The Startup Thesis
Cybersecurity defense in emerging markets cannot rely on paid consumer subscriptions because everyday citizens (especially vulnerable elders and students) cannot afford or understand antivirus software.

**KROH operates on a Dual-Engine Model:**
1. **B2C Public Utility (100% Free):** Free Telegram shield for 17 million Cambodians to maximize viral adoption, data moat, and national brand trust.
2. **B2B Enterprise Threat Intelligence Feed (Recurring Revenue):** Commercial banks, telcos, and payment providers pay monthly retainers to receive real-time, zero-day threat feeds intercepted by KROH.

---

## 2. Revenue Streams

### A. B2B Enterprise Threat Intelligence API ($500 – $2,500 / month)
* **Target Customers:**
  * Commercial Banks: ABA Bank, Wing Bank, ACLEDA, Sathapana, Canadia.
  * Mobile Telcos: Smart Axiata, Cellcard, Metfone.
  * E-Commerce & Delivery: Nham24, FoodPanda Cambodia.
* **Value Proposition:**
  * Banks currently discover phishing sites targeting their brand 2 to 3 weeks after launch (via customer complaints).
  * KROH detects new phishing domains (`aba-bonus.top`, `wing-cash.cc`) within **15 minutes** of them being blasted into Telegram groups.
  * Banks pay to receive automated webhook alerts with Whois, registrar, and DNS IP data to execute immediate takedown notices with MPTC.

### B. MPTC Anti-Cybercrime & National Security Grants ($10,000 – $50,000)
* Cambodia’s Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC) funds digital resilience initiatives aligned with the *Digital Economy and Social Policy Framework 2021–2035*.
